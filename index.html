questions

The ones people ask.

Does Cue text people for me automatically?

No, and it cannot. iOS does not let any app send a message without you. Cue writes the draft and opens Messages with it loaded. You press send.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign up, no password. Open it and start adding people.

Where do my contacts and notes live?

On your iPhone. Cue asks for Contacts only when you choose to import, and keeps only who you pick. The privacy policy has the full detail.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. Cue keeps reminding you about up to five people and keeps drafting on your device. Subscribe whenever you want the rest back.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Cue. Apple handles billing, so cancelling there stops it everywhere. There is a link inside the app too.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Cue leans on iOS notifications and the native Messages composer, so iPhone came first.
