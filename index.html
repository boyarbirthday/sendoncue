the honest part

Nothing goes out behind your back.

iPhone will not let any app text for you, and Cue would not want to. Every text goes through your own Messages, from your own number, after you have read it. That is why it lands like you wrote it. Because you did.

And your people stay yours. The circle, the dates, the sample texts you paste in: all of it lives on your phone. With AI drafting on, Cue sends only what one text needs to get written, and keeps none of it on a server.

questions

The ones people ask.

Does Cue text people for me automatically?

No, and it cannot. iOS does not let any app send a message without you. Cue writes the text and opens Messages with it loaded. You press send.

Where does everything live?

On your iPhone. Your circle, their dates, your notes and the texts you paste in are all saved on your phone. Cue asks for Contacts only when you choose to import, and keeps only who you pick. The privacy policy has the full detail.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. Cue keeps reminding you about up to five people and keeps drafting on your device. Subscribe whenever you want the rest back.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Cue leans on iOS notifications and the native Messages composer, so iPhone came first.
