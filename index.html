the honest part

The AI drafts. You press send.

iPhone will not allow any app to send a text for you, and Cue would not want to. The AI drafts, Messages opens, and you press send from your own number. That is why every text lands like you wrote it. Because you did.

Your people stay yours. The circle, the dates, the sample texts: all of it lives on your phone. When the AI drafts a text, it uses only what that one text needs, and keeps nothing on a server.

Set Cue up once, and the right words arrive on the right day. Being thoughtful stops taking willpower.

questions

The ones people ask.

Does Cue text people for me automatically?

No, and it cannot. iOS does not allow any app to send a message without you. The AI drafts the text and opens Messages with it loaded. You press send.

Where does everything live?

On your iPhone. Your circle, their dates, your notes and the texts you paste in are all saved on your phone. Cue asks for Contacts only when you choose to import, and keeps only who you pick. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Do I need an account?

Yes, a light one: your email and a phone number. We email you a code to sign in; there is no password. Your people still live on your phone, not on the account.

What does Cue cost?

Cue is free to try for two weeks, the whole thing. After the trial, you can explore a few options to keep Cue working for you: monthly, yearly, or the free circle of up to five people. Billing runs through your Apple Account, and nothing you have added is ever deleted.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Cue leans on iOS notifications and the native Messages composer, so iPhone came first.
